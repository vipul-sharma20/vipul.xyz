Came back to Bengaluru after Diwali week and found that my room's lights had stopped working.

I had two 5-metre LED strips lying around that I'd previously used in my living room, controlled by a Raspberry Pi.

I also had a spare ESP32, so instead of fixing my lights, I spent time hooking everything up and asking Codex to write and flash the firmware for my LED strip setup.

Here's a video of my partially complete setup with the song requested by my friends.

_PS: don't judge me on the mess or cable management, only I know how I keep this house functional with all the [jugaads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jugaad)_
