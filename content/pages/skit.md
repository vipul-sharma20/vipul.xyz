## Work

I worked at [Skit.ai][skit.ai] for 8.5 years, from March, 2018 - October, 2026.
I started as a Founding Engineer who started working on building voice bots
when it was pivoting from chat bots to voice bots. Back then it was known as [Vernacular.ai][vernacular.ai].

I can confidently claim to have the best pre-joining story in the entire
company. Since this page sits in public domain, I won't elaborate on that
(nothing illegal). You should find about it from people joined in 2018 or
before.

I was originally responsible for building the first telephony platform between
2018-2020 and taking it live with all the customers in that time period. Since
then, the platform has changed completely and more competent people own it now.

In 2021, I started working in the ML Platform team, worked on a few ML
services, internal tools for our ML stack and MLOps initiatives.

In late 2021/early-2022, I moved to working on the Dialogue Manager along with
a few other members in the team which eventually was named as "Core
Services"and I got a chance to lead this team. 2022 was also the time when I
worked on starting up the incident management process which still continues to
operate in of course in a much better way because of all the people who have
refined it further.

2023 was the time when intelligence was commoditized in form of OpenAI's APIs
and I along with a few people started working on setting up this new
orchestration/runtime. This was the time when there were architectural decision
conflicts, a lot of decision making around build vs buy and fundamentally
questioning about how Skit.ai should think about building voice bots.

With the help of all the talented folks (a lot of them who have moved on since)
we were able incrementally built the LLM bot which over time were colloquially
known as v0, v1, v2, v3.

In 2024 & 2025, post the exit of a few Engineering leaders, I got the
opportunity to take up a bigger role that involved managing a 10+ team of
engineers. I got to work much more with the Delivery and Product team along
with shaping up engineering initiatives and further iterate over the runtime
systems.

## Why Leave?

In 2026 before I decided to leave, I could see myself no longer fitting well
with the team/culture what Skit.ai required at the time and I couldn't align
myself myself with the changes and decisions being made. It felt disrespectful
to the people who were contributing with their full intent while I couldn't
make myself to show the same commitment towards the cause.

I won't dwell a lot on specifics of things that didn't go well but would like
to celebrate the work that I was able to put in all these years and how I have
better myself.

## Where Next?

I leave Skit.ai without any next full-time job with hopes of figuring out the
kind of org/people/culture I would want to work with in the coming
weeks/months.

Some people skydive for the thrill but I get it by quitting my job without a
next one with an active home loan liabilities.
