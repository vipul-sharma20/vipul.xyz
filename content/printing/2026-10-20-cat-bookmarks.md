Credits to [Stox3D][stox] for their [cat bookmark model][stox-model] and [jonas.oriovits][jonas] for their [cat bookmark model][jonas-model]

I 3D printed these bookmarks for my wife. The one on the left was printed with
black and white filament, with a single filament change, so it looks cleaner
than the other one. The one on the right is all black and I painted its eyes
white by hand.
