Credits to [Tifferzzz][maker] for their [ADHD checklist keychain model][model]

A keychain-sized checklist with sliding checkboxes that click into place. There
is a paper strip that you can slide between the frame so you can pull it out
and rewrite it.

The top and bottom frame assembly is a little loose so it gets dismantled
easily if you flip it around. For that I have put a thin transparent tape along
the edges on one side.
